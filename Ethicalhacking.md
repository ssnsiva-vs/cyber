## Concept 2

### Definitions

1. Cyber asset - Connected IT assets accessible over the network. (Hardware, Software, Network, Application, Data)
2. Cyber threats - Tactics, techniques and procedures that can compromise the security posture of a cyber asset.
3. Vulnerabilities - Inherent weaknesses in a cyber asset permitting exploitation by the cyber threats.
